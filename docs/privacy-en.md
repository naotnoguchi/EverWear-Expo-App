# Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 22, 2025

EverWear Office ("we", "us", or "our") respects the privacy of users ("Users") of the mobile application "EverWear" (the "App"). This Privacy Policy ("Policy") explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use the App.

## 1. Information We Collect
We may collect the following information necessary to provide and improve the App:

- Information provided by Users, such as email address entered in registration forms
- Usage information (logs, device information, interaction history, etc.)
- Any other information that Users voluntarily enter in forms specified by us

## 2. Purpose of Use
We use the collected information for the following purposes:

1. To provide, maintain, and improve the App and related services
2. To verify identity, respond to inquiries, and provide user support
3. To inform Users of new features, updates, and promotional campaigns
4. To notify Users of important changes such as amendments to the Terms of Service or this Policy
5. To create and analyze statistical data to improve the App

## 3. Provision to Third Parties
Except as permitted by laws and regulations, we do not provide personal information to third parties without User consent.

## 4. Information Management
We implement appropriate security measures to protect personal information from unauthorized access, loss, destruction, alteration, and leakage.

## 5. Cookies and Similar Technologies
We do not currently use cookies, advertising identifiers, or analytics tools. Should we decide to introduce such technologies in the future, we will update this Policy and disclose the necessary information appropriately.

## 6. User Rights
Users may request disclosure, correction, deletion, or suspension of use of their personal information in accordance with applicable laws. For procedures, please contact us as set forth in Section 8.

## 7. Amendments
We may revise this Policy in response to changes in laws or service content. The revised Policy will take effect when posted on the App or otherwise made public by us.
